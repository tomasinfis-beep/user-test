FAQ

Fast answers.

Do I pay to submit a case?

No. The beta review is free. The success fee only applies to value actually recovered under an agreed arrangement.

What if the merchant says no?

We record the outcome and, where appropriate, identify the next legitimate route. There is no guarantee of recovery.

What should I not send?

Never send passwords, full payment details, authentication codes or government identification.
